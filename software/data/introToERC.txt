About ERC

The Electronics and Robotics Club (ERC) is a student club at BITS Pilani Goa campus. ERC members have interests ranging from electronics and machine learning to mechanical design.

ERC was founded in 2008 and is one of the oldest clubs on the BITS Goa campus. It has grown into a platform for learning and experimenting with science and engineering, and applying them in robotics.

ERC welcomes electronics engineers, mechanical enthusiasts and coders. Members are organised into teams working on hardware design, open source libraries, and full robots built from scratch.

ERC holds weekly discussion sessions, demonstrations and presentations where members share their experience and expertise.

Over the years, ERC has shifted from competing in robotics competitions to research-oriented projects with real world applications. ERC tries to keep up with the latest developments in robotics research and industry.

ERC also builds exhibition projects, which are displayed at the annual technical fest on campus and at other events through the year.

ERC is open to anyone with a general interest in engineering who wants to explore robotics. Anyone interested can get in touch with the club.
